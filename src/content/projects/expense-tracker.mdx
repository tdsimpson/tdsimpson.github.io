<Section id="what" heading="What I made">

Expense Tracker is a straightforward web app that lets you add, edit, and delete purchase data so you can categorize and visualize your spending in charts and graphs (powered by Chart.js). It turns a month’s data into something legible at a glance. You can search or filter by category, or view a specific period of time. It’s built with React, Redux, Firebase, and SCSS for the styles.

<Section id="why" heading="Why I built it">

I built Expense Tracker because I wanted a simple way to visualize my spending without connecting it to my bank account. I didn’t like the charts my bank itself gave me, and I didn’t want to hand my data to a third-party app for privacy reasons. I built a version where I entered things manually so I could see my data the way I want to see it. It also gave me a real project to get hands-on practice with React, Redux, and Firebase.
